# Catalogue statistics (Grim Dawn 1.3.0.8)

Generated by `tools/build_catalogue.py` from the game's own database (set GD_DIR, or let it find Steam).
Merged database: 82448 records over database (34182), gdx1 (18453), gdx2 (16454), gdx3 (24456); 20399 English text tags.

## The collection rule

Two rules decide what the mod's tab has a box for:

- **equipment** (`ArmorProtective_*`, `ArmorJewelry_*`, `Weapon*`): `itemClassification`
  `Epic` or `Legendary`, and nothing else.
- **relics** (`Class = ItemArtifact`, i.e. `records/items/gearrelic/*`): **every**
  classification. The b-series relics are `Rare`
  records but they are unique-tier items to a player, and they were the only relic tier
  the tab had no box for.

| relic classification | records | file prefix |
|---|---:|---|
| Rare | 21 | `b*_relic*.dbr` |
| Epic | 21 | `c*_relic*.dbr` |
| Legendary | 49 | `d*_relic*.dbr` |
| **total relics** | **91** | `records/items/gearrelic/` |

`build_catalogue.py` ASSERTS those three counts (`RELIC_EXPECT`), that every accepted
relic sits under `records/items/gearrelic/`, that every relic bitmap resolves in
`resources/Items.arc`, and that equipment + relics comes to `COLLECTION_EXPECT_TOTAL`.
A game patch that changes any of them fails the build loudly; the fix is to re-read the
counts and update the constants, never to silence the assert.

- **curated inclusions** (`EXTRA_RECORDS`): a hand-written list of records
  the shipped filter drops but a player can really own. It bypasses the four rules below
  and is asserted the same way (the record must exist, its name tag must resolve, its
  bitmap must be in `resources/Items.arc`).

| curated inclusion | class | classification | group | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `records/endlessdungeon/items/a001_ring.dbr` (Leovinus' Ring) | `ArmorJewelry_Ring` | Common | ring | Shattered Realm named reward a player can own but the shipped filter drops |

`EXTRA_EXCLUDE` names the developer test records in the same folder that must NEVER be
collectible, and the build asserts none of them reaches the collection:

- `records/endlessdungeon/items/q001_torso.dbr`
- `records/endlessdungeon/items/z001_test.dbr`

## Shipped-record filter (unchanged)

A raw scan of the merged database finds far more Epic/Legendary records than grimtools lists.
Four rules bring the two into agreement; each was verified by inspecting the records it drops.
They apply to relics too - they are what keeps the 12 `records/sandbox/jakub/**` test relics out.

| # | Rule | What it removes |
|---|------|-----------------|
| 1 | path starts with `records/items/` | dev/test items under `records/sandbox/**` (44 Epic+Leg equipment records, e.g. `records/sandbox/jim/legendary sets/...`), `records/storyelements/signs/signh.dbr`, `records/endlessdungeon/items/z001_test.dbr` |
| 2 | path not under `records/items/enemygear/` | monster-worn gear (`gear_witchgod_boss01_head.dbr` etc.), never dropped as loot |
| 3 | the record's name tag resolves in Text_EN | records whose `itemNameTag` / `description` is a dangling tag |
| 4 | `FileDescription` contains no "BLANK" | the designer template records, exactly 4 per equipment slot per classification: `c000/c100/c200/c300_axe2h.dbr` all say `BASE BLANK EPIC 2H AXE`, share tag `tagWeaponMelee2hC000` and sit at level 1 |

Name tag field: `itemNameTag` for equipment classes, `description` for every other item class
(`ItemArtifact`, `ItemRelic`, `ItemEnchantment`, `ItemArtifactFormula`, ...).
Bitmap field: `bitmap`, except `ItemArtifact` -> `artifactBitmap` and `ItemRelic` -> `relicBitmap`.

### Remaining per-slot differences

The two headline sums agree (Epic +1, Legendary -1, sum exactly 3,196), but a few slots still
disagree by a handful of records. They cancel out, which points at grimtools filing some items
under a different slot than the record's `Class` (its class ids c10..c49 are its own taxonomy),
not at extra or missing records here. The biggest single gap is daggers (-6 Legendary) against
scepters/off-hands/amulets being over; every one of the extra records here has a real name, a
real bitmap present in Items.arc and a level requirement. Not resolved; grimtools has no public
API to diff item-by-item against, so per-slot equality could not be checked directly.

## Monster Infrequents and unique rares - NOT reliably separable

No DBR field marks a Monster Infrequent. What exists in the database is:

- Ordinary Rare loot is generated at runtime: a Common base record plus a `LootRandomizer`
  prefix/suffix. Such items have no record of their own.
- Every equipment record whose `itemClassification` is `Rare` is therefore a *fixed* rare -
  a Monster Infrequent, a quest/boss 'unique rare', or a faction-store item.
- The only clean split inside that group is the path (and the matching filename prefix):
  `records/items/faction/**` = `f*` files, 466 records, all with `itemCost`
  and `soulbound`; everything else = `b*` files, 2443 records.
- The game does have an MI concept - the loot filter has a `Monster Infrequent` checkbox
  (`tagLootFilter04`) described as "items that drop from specific enemies" - but that
  classification is made in engine code from the monster loot tables, not from a record field.

| bucket | records | distinct names |
|---|---:|---:|
| `records/items/**` Rare equipment, non-faction (MI + unique rares) | 2443 | 500 |
| `records/items/faction/**` Rare equipment (faction store) | 466 | 257 |
| grimtools 'Monster Infrequents' | 2489 | 562 (unverified) |
| grimtools 'Unique rare items' | 66 | |

So the non-faction bucket (2443) is 112 short of grimtools' MI + unique-rare total
(2489 + 66 = 2555), and adding the faction bucket overshoots it
(2909). Two heuristics were tried and rejected:
names with exactly one record (72 hits, and their slot spread - 9 belts, 8 off-hands, 0 rings -
does not match the researcher's unique-rare spread of 10 rings / 4 amulets / 1 medal), and
`itemStyleTag` (it carries cosmetic style words like Mythical/Empowered/Elite, not a rarity role).
**`uniqueRares` in catalogue.json is therefore `null`, not a guess.** `monsterInfrequents`
holds the whole non-faction Rare-record bucket and is a superset of the real MI list.
Resolving this properly means walking the monster loot tables, which is out of scope for M3.

## Icons

Rule (verified, now also in ENGINE-FACTS): a record's bitmap field is a *resource* path whose
first segment names the archive and is **not** part of the archive entry name -
`items/gearhead/bitmaps/d026_head.tex` is entry `gearhead/bitmaps/d026_head.tex` in `resources/Items.arc`,
`ui/caravan/caravan_tabup.tex` is entry `caravan/caravan_tabup.tex` in `resources/UI.arc`.
The four copies of each archive (base, gdx1, gdx2, gdx3) stack in the same load order as the .arz files.

- Epic/Legendary entries with a bitmap: 4049 / 4088
- of those, the .tex resource was found in Items.arc / UI.arc: 4049 (missing: 0)
- entries with no bitmap field at all: 39 (ItemArtifactFormula)
